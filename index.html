Welcome to the Law Office of Allison M. Kourbage

Buying or selling a home is one of the most significant financial decisions most people will ever make. Our firm concentrates on real estate transactions and related legal services, helping clients navigate the purchase, sale, and leasing of residential and commercial property from contract to closing.

Located in Melville, New York, we represent clients throughout Long Island, including Huntington, Huntington Station, Dix Hills, Melville, and the surrounding communities in Nassau and Suffolk County.

Every transaction receives the same careful attention: contracts are reviewed thoroughly, questions are answered promptly and in plain English, and problems are addressed before they can delay your closing. Our goal is a smooth, efficient process for every client.

We invite you to call the office at (631) 427-5455 to discuss your transaction.

Our Practice Areas

The firm handles all aspects of residential and commercial real estate transactions on Long Island.

Buyer Representation

Review and negotiation of the contract of sale, guidance through inspections, mortgage contingencies and title review, and representation at your closing.

Seller Representation

Preparation of the contract of sale, negotiation of terms, resolution of title issues, and coordination with brokers and the purchaser's counsel through closing.

Leases & Landlord–Tenant

Drafting and review of residential and commercial leases for landlords and tenants, with practical advice on rights and obligations under New York law.

Contract Review & Closings

Careful preparation and review of all transaction documents, coordination with lenders and title companies, and steady representation at the closing table.
