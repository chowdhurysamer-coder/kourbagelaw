About the Firm

Counsel that treats your transaction like it's the only one.

Located in Melville, New York, the Law Office of Allison M. Kourbage focuses on real estate transactions and related legal services. Whether you are buying your first home, selling a longtime residence, or negotiating a lease, the firm helps you navigate the process with clarity and confidence.

Known for expertise and attention to detail, the firm's goal is simple: a smooth, efficient process for every client — with straight answers, careful document review, and an advocate who is personally invested in your outcome.

  • Personalized guidance from contract to closing
  • Meticulous review of every document you sign
  • Responsive, plain-English communication
  • Conveniently located in Melville, serving Long Island
